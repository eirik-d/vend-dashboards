Summary

Three-card T1 snapshot from the Real Estate leadership team.

What's good

  • Finalized the Marketplace transition for FINN and on-boarded the first customers on new pro platform
  • High user engagement of new AI search capabilities (NEMO) and interactive floorplan tools. Redecoration tool shows promising results.
  • Achieved strong initial traction with HomeQ Samla, scaling to 15,000 paying subscribers within the first six months.
  • Growth outlook remains highly ambitious, maintaining a +30% YoY revenue target for Oikotie heading into 2027.

What's bad

  • Qasa Norway performance is tracking significantly behind the ambitious volume and revenue targets set for T1, mainly due to low top funnel conversion
  • Competitive market momentum in Finland is being actively challenged by aggressive, heavy spending from rivals.

What's top of mind

  • Etuovi is ramping up in marketing, requiring an tactical response and strategy realignment for Oikotie.
  • Developing and scaling targeted strategy plans to remove upper-funnel friction and clearly differentiate user roles between FINN and Qasa.
  • Finalizing the upcoming 2027 monetization and packaging models to capture projected growth in FINN and Oikotie
  • FINN execution is dependent on product sales verticalization, and PTX core support on search
  • Super Mario and the developing the next Strategy for Real Estate vertical requires high management capacity

Demand

Per-listing views are down, but this is a supply effect — not a demand problem. New listings were up 8% YoY in April, and more ads competing for the same audience naturally dilutes average first-week views. The pattern is visible in the seasonal dips: when supply drops over summer and Christmas, per-ad views spike inversely. Total engagement is actually up across the board — favorites +7.6%, agent leads +15.7%, and viewing signups +53.2% YoY. The viewing surge is likely driven by the new open house reminder notification.

Qasa Norway — status & plan

Norway is the market with highest share of C2C rentals in the Nordics and therefore the market with the largest potential. Qasa Norway is a strong launch compared to previous market entries, but we have clearly overestimated the initial growth pace and are tracking significantly behind our ambitious targets.

The main challenge is in the upper funnel

Recap: We made a major decision to utilise the core FINN brand and not build Qasa as a stand-alone marketplace brand. In the current solution, Qasa as an option is only visible in the ad insertion for new ads or republishing within the rental category.

Listings continue to be the biggest challenge, with too low opt-in for Qasa in the ad insertion. Once inside the Qasa funnel, the conversion rate is high — the highest of all three markets.

We have good insights into key barriers to adoption and why many landlords are not yet choosing Qasa.

Price is perceived as a blocker — several users mention paying for the ad and the service fee
The Qasa brand is still unknown and the value proposition is not always well understood
Users get the impression they need to choose between Qasa and FINN. FINN is then perceived as the 'safer' bet
Some segments perceive the Qasa service as 'too heavy' for their needs
The plan to fuel growth

We have looked into changes across the 5Ps (price, product, people, placements and promotion) to leave no stone unturned. Many initiatives are on their way, and teams within both Qasa and FINN are collaborating well.

Our two biggest bets:

"Trygg leie"
Launching a new way of entering the Qasa value proposition later in the funnel — for both tenant and landlord. Will be visible on all private ads on FINN.no during May.
Clearer communication of value proposition
Clearer roles between FINN and Qasa: "You find the tenant on FINN. Qasa will take care of the rest." Qasa solution better integrated in the FINN ad insertion funnel at relevant touch points.
